Budget notes for deepseek-bindings.example.json / DeepSeek 绑定示例预算说明

At peak rates with long_write.max_output_tokens set to 12000, each in-flight
Pro long review reserves about US$0.06–0.07. Set the background lane to at least
US$0.07 per concurrent long review, or about US$0.50 for six cards. The reservation
is an upper bound. Observed settlement was about US$0.005–0.018 per review.
Pro calls took about 20–70 seconds in the smoke test. An execution defaults to
a 120-second time limit.

按示例的 long_write.max_output_tokens 12000 与高峰费率，每篇在途 Pro 长评
预留约 0.06–0.07 美元。后台车道至少按「同时生成的长评数 × 0.07 美元」配置，
6 张卡可按约 0.50 美元准备。预留是上限，实测单篇结算约 0.005–0.018 美元。
冒烟中 Pro 单次调用约 20–70 秒，单次执行的缺省时限为 120 秒。
